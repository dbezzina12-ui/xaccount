HOW TO PLAY

01

25 LINES. LEFT TO RIGHT.

Match three or more symbols on consecutive reels along a payline, starting on reel 1. Matches cannot skip a reel or start in the middle. Only the highest win pays on each of the 25 fixed lines. Each spin pays once. Super Outbreak remembers each zombie’s starting row between spins.

02

CARDS & HUMANS

Four card ranks: J, Q, K, A. Five humans: Skater, Medic, Builder, Officer, Scientist. Zombies and their wild trails substitute for these symbols. Each character has a human form and a fully zombified form. Zombified characters blink and move their mouths, and still match their usual human symbol on paylines. Canisters trigger Outbreak and do not substitute.

03

WALK & EAT

In the base game and regular Outbreak, a zombie chooses one straight direction: up, down, left or right. It eats consecutive humans, moves into their positions and leaves ordinary wilds behind. Cards, canisters, other wilds and the edge stop it. No diagonal movement or turns. Each normal human adds +1×. In the base game, zombified characters add either +3× or +5×. Each still takes just one step. The added value appears when they are eaten. Two normal humans give 2×; a +3× zombified character and a +5× zombified character give 8×.

04

THE GROWING ZOMBIE

After any vertical walk, the zombie grows across its starting position and every step as one continuous character. One step makes it 2 tall; two steps make it 3 tall; three steps make it 4 tall. Height depends on positions traveled, not multiplier value. A full reel uses all five positions and combines that reel’s zombie multipliers.

05

ONE MULTIPLIER PER ZOMBIE

Every position inside the tall artwork carries that zombie’s multiplier. Each winning line counts that zombie once. Horizontal zombies stay one position tall; their trail has no added multiplier. When a winning line passes through multiple multiplied zombies, their multipliers add once each. Base-game, Outbreak and Super multipliers reset every spin. Hidden Outbreak keeps each reel’s multiplier for the remaining free spins, adds new bites to it, then resets when the bonus ends.

06

OUTBREAK · THREE EATS TO UNLOCK

Exactly three canister scatters anywhere award eight Outbreak free spins with more frequent zombies. Five glass tubes beside the reels on desktop, or below on mobile, track each human type. Each eat raises the green goo to the next of three markers. The character stays human through the first two eats; the third turns its portrait and matching reel symbols fully zombie and unlocks that type’s multiplier for the rest of this bonus. Those first three eats each add +1×. Subsequent bites collect the value below, including matching humans still on the current board. Humans keep their usual line pays. Infection resets after the bonus.

07

SUPER OUTBREAK · RETURN & RISE

Four canister scatters anywhere award eight Super free spins. All humans start infected at the values above. Once a zombie lands, that reel remembers its starting row for the remaining bonus. On every next spin it returns to that same row, unless it upgrades to a lower row. It never moves its starting point higher. Row numbers count down from 1 at the top to 5 at the bottom.

Every reel spins fresh symbols. Each returning zombie starts with no multiplier and walks upward to the top again, turning crossed positions into wilds. Humans add their values; cards add nothing. A zombie starting on row 3 becomes three tall each spin, or taller after an upgrade. A bottom-row zombie repeats its full five-position climb every spin. Only its starting row carries over; multipliers and eaten counts are rebuilt each time and can vary.

New zombie landings or lower-row upgrades can occur while a reel can still improve. All previously established zombies return regardless. A top-row landing stays a one-position wild until upgraded.

08

HIDDEN OUTBREAK · KEEP EVERY BITE

Five or more canister scatters award eight Hidden free spins. It plays like Super: humans start infected, zombies return to their starting row or upgrade lower, and climb upward each spin. Each reel also keeps its own collected multiplier. New bites add to that reel’s saved value: 12× plus a 5× bite becomes 17×, then the next spin starts from 17×. Cards add nothing. New reels start at zero. Multipliers stay separate by reel, count once per zombie on a winning line, and reset when the bonus ends. Hidden can also be unlocked through Mystery Buy; it has no direct buy.

Zombies can land during normal spins and all bonus tiers. Buy Outbreak for 100× base bet, Super Outbreak for 250×, or Mystery Outbreak for 250×. Mystery reveals one bonus: 40% regular Outbreak, 50% Super or 10% Hidden. The tier is chosen once when the purchase is confirmed. All three bonus tiers award eight spins with no retriggers. Buy prices use free-play credits. A round ends early if it reaches the 25,000× limit.

09

SECOND LIFE · HEART RESPINS

Activate Second Life to use it on every manual or autoplay spin for 10× base bet per paid spin. It stays on until disabled, and replaces Enhanced Spins while active. The controller shows the full spin cost. Two zombies are guaranteed, and each chooses a straight direction: up, down, left or right. A heart must be reached along that path to award one respin; cards, other zombies and wild trails still block the walk. Hearts add no multiplier and do not pay on lines. Up to one heart can appear per spin.

For an awarded respin, both zombies return to their original starting positions with their own collected multipliers. Other symbols and old wild trails refresh, and new directions are chosen. Hearts can retrigger. Each spin pays once; all spin wins are added into one feature total. The initial paid spin costs 10× base bet and all heart respins are included. Multipliers reset before the next paid spin. Activate Second Life between rounds in Bonuses & Feature Spins or the game menu. The Bonus Buy button becomes Disable while active; press it to return to normal spins. This feature does not contain FS scatters.

Enhanced Spins cost 3× base bet per spin. Any bonus triggers on average once in 50 Enhanced spins, compared with once in 275 normal spins. Each natural bonus is 90% Outbreak, 9% Super and 1% Hidden. These are long-term probabilities, not a countdown or guarantee. The displayed Enhanced bet is the full spin cost. Wins and the 25,000× round limit are calculated from the base bet. Enhanced Spins do not change bonus-buy prices or charge extra for free spins. Switch them on or off in Bonuses & Feature Spins or the game menu, between rounds.

Winning lines are drawn across the reels. Different paylines can use the same symbol, but each line pays only its highest result. All-wild matches can pay from reel 1. The complete round is capped at 25,000× bet.

Skip finishes only the current spin. Each following free spin uses your selected Normal, Quick or Turbo speed. Autoplay stops at a bonus; stopping autoplay lets the current round finish.

Calibrated free-play prototype: 96% theoretical RTP in each paid mode, measured against its full cost. This is not independently certified. The 25,000× maximum is reachable in every paid mode; normal-spin max-win frequency is approximately 1 in 7.5 million complete rounds. Credits retain fractional cents internally; displayed amounts are rounded to two decimals. All Walk Demo modes, Full Reel and bonus tests are feature previews and leave credits unchanged.
