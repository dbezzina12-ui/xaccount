## Rules as the trial engine settles them

- **Board.** The same 6×5 pay-anywhere tumbles. There are no stamps and no collection jobs.
- **Base and Priority Mail spins** first draw a rack of 2 basics. Each paying group of a racked basic adds +1 to that spin's meter, which starts at 1× and multiplies the spin's tumble subtotal once. Their FS chance is fitted for 96%.
- **The rack.** Each bonus draws six symbols: three basics, two of PHONE, PARCEL and ENVELOPE, and KEY. **Every bonus racks KEY.** The rack is fixed for the whole bonus.
- **The meter (capped ladder).** Each racked basic or premium that pays adds its next addition, which then doubles, but only up to +2 for basics and +6 for premiums (basics +1, then +2; premiums +3, then +6). KEY no longer multiplies the meter. The meter carries through the bonus and multiplies each spin's tumble subtotal once.
- **The key ring.** Every KEY that lands in a bonus, on the first board or in a refill, turns one notch. Each full ring of 5 notches adds to the meter: Night Shift +3, Double Post +6, Lost & Found +25. Notches carry across free spins.
- **The vault.** The first KEY match (8 or more KEYs paying) opens the vault: the meter goes to its ceiling and stays there for the rest of the bonus.
  - **Night Shift** starts at 3× and applies only on a spin with a rack hit, a ring fill or an open vault.
  - **Double Post** starts at 15× and applies every spin.
  - **Lost & Found** starts at 40× and applies every spin.
  - Ceilings are 60× / 100× / 1000×.
  - **Lost & Found pays at least 1,000×.** A bonus that ends below it is topped up to it.
- **Free-spin landings (fitted).** Night Shift basics ×0.7798 and KEY weight 4, Double Post basics ×0.7831 and KEY weight 4, Lost & Found basics ×1.3495 and KEY weight 7. Mystery Delivery draws regular 0%, super 90%, hidden 10%.

## Findings for the producer

- **KEY acts in almost every bonus.** The ring fills at least once in 99.1% of Night Shift, 99.1% of Double Post and 100.0% of Lost & Found bonuses (2.0, 2.0 and 2.9 fills on average).
- **Buys:** Night Shift (100×) pays back at least its price in 34% of buys and less than half of it in 39%; Double Post (250×) pays back at least its price in 37% of buys and less than half of it in 30%.
- **The vault is the max-win path.** A KEY match opens it in 1 of every 479 Lost & Found bonuses, and Lost & Found reaches the cap 1 in 517. Night Shift and Double Post open it 1 in 4,444 and 1 in 4,587.
- **Lost & Found:** 63% of bonuses pass 1,000× by themselves, and the 1,000× minimum pays the other 37%. The median bonus pays 1,248.6×.
- **Night Shift applies its meter on 41% of its spins** (29% have a rack hit; the rest of them a ring fill or an open vault). The others pay face value.
- **Golden seeds** are in `tests/six.test.mjs`.
