## Rules as the trial engine settles them

- **Board.** The same 6×5 pay-anywhere tumbles. There are no stamps and no collection jobs.
- **Base and Priority Mail spins** first draw a rack of 2 basics. Each paying group of a racked basic doubles that spin's meter, which starts at 1× and multiplies the spin's tumble subtotal once. Their FS chance is fitted for 96%.
- **The rack.** Each bonus draws six symbols: three basics, two of PHONE, PARCEL and ENVELOPE, and KEY. **Every bonus racks KEY.** The rack is fixed for the whole bonus.
- **The meter.** Each racked basic or premium that pays adds to the meter: Night Shift basics add nothing, premiums +1; Double Post basics add nothing, premiums +1; Lost & Found basics +1, then +2, premiums +3, then +6. KEY never multiplies. The meter carries through the bonus and multiplies each spin's tumble subtotal once.
- **The key ring.** Every KEY that lands in a bonus, on the first board or in a refill, turns one notch. Each full ring (Night Shift 7, Double Post 7 and Lost & Found 5 notches) adds to the meter: Night Shift +1, Double Post +1, Lost & Found +20. Notches carry across free spins.
- **The vault.** The first KEY match (8 or more KEYs paying) opens the vault: the meter goes to its ceiling (Night Shift 60×, Double Post 120×, Lost & Found 1,000×) and stays there. In Night Shift and Double Post a second KEY match opens the **inner vault**: 1,000× for the rest of the bonus.
  - **Night Shift** starts at 2× and applies only on a spin with a rack hit, a ring fill or an open vault.
  - **Double Post** starts at 5× and applies every spin.
  - **Lost & Found** starts at 30× and applies every spin.
  - Ceilings are 60× / 120× / 1000×.
  - **Lost & Found pays at least 1,000×.** A bonus that ends below it is topped up to it.
- **Free-spin landings (fitted).** Night Shift basics ×1.8102 and KEY weight 8.5, Double Post basics ×2.12 and KEY weight 9, Lost & Found basics ×1.4908 and KEY weight 8.5. Mystery Delivery draws regular 0%, super 90%, hidden 10%.

- **KEY acts in almost every bonus.** The ring fills at least once in 99.6% of Night Shift, 99.5% of Double Post and 100.0% of Lost & Found bonuses (2.1, 2.1 and 3.4 fills on average).
- **Buys:** Night Shift (100×) pays back at least its price in 32% of buys and less than half of it in 23%; Double Post (250×) pays back at least its price in 33% of buys and less than half of it in 19%.
- **Max wins** (4,000,000 more bonuses each): Night Shift reaches the cap 1 in 285,714 (inner vault 1 in 285,714), Double Post 1 in 571,429 (inner vault 1 in 571,429). Their first vault opens 1 in 360 and 1 in 384: that is where their biggest ordinary hits come from (0.35% of Night Shift pay 500×+, 0.37% of Double Post pay 1,000×+).
- **The vault is the max-win path.** A KEY match opens it in 1 of every 203 Lost & Found bonuses, and Lost & Found reaches the cap 1 in 215. Night Shift and Double Post open it 1 in 360 and 1 in 384.
- **Lost & Found:** 59% of bonuses pass 1,000× by themselves, and the 1,000× minimum pays the other 41%. The median bonus pays 1,178.6×.
- **Night Shift applies its meter on 59% of its spins** (52% have a rack hit; the rest of them a ring fill or an open vault). The others pay face value.
