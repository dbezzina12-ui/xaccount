## Rules as the trial engine settles them

- **Board.** The same 6×5 pay-anywhere tumbles. There are no stamps and no collection jobs.
- **Base and Priority Mail spins** first draw a rack of 2 basics. Each paying group of a racked basic doubles that spin's multiplier (from 1×). Their FS chance is fitted for 96%.
- **Key ring and vault.** Every bonus has a 5-notch key ring. Every KEY that lands (first board or refill) turns a notch, and notches carry across spins. Each full ring opens the vault: it reveals a multiplier from the bonus's table, which adds to the bonus multiplier for the rest of the bonus. The multiplier applies to every spin.
- **Starting multiplier.** Night Shift 2×, Double Post 5×. Lost & Found opens a vault before its first spin, from its opening table.
- **Perks** (drawn when the bonus starts): Night Shift 2 of 4 at random; Double Post and Lost & Found all 4.
  - **Build:** 2 basics are marked; each paying group of one turns a key-ring notch.
  - **Return:** 3+ ENVELOPEs on the board: the spin's wins pay twice when it ends.
  - **Unpack:** 5+ PARCELs on the board turn four more cells into the board's most common premium.
  - **Deliver:** 2+ PHONEs on the board bring in the symbols missing from the nearest group.
  - Each premium perk fires at most once a spin and has 3 charges a bonus in Night Shift and Lost & Found, 2 in Double Post. Build is not limited.
- **Lost & Found pays at least 1,000×.** A bonus that ends below it is topped up to it.
- **Free-spin landings (fitted).** Night Shift basics ×1.102, KEY 4; Double Post basics ×1.0159, KEY 3.5; Lost & Found basics ×1.4392, KEY 6. Mystery Delivery draws regular 0%, super 90%, hidden 10%.
