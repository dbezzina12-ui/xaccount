## How a round settles

- **The sorter.** It has 5 stages × 3 lanes, with lane 0 at the top. A spin's letter enters the middle lane. Each cell holds one stamp:
  - **Up**, **Straight** or **Down** moves the letter one lane into the next stage.
  - **Fork** sends one copy up and one copy down.
  - **Jam** kills that branch.
  - A move past the top or bottom lane is lost at the edge.
  - Copies that arrive in the same lane are reunited and count once.
- **Delivery.** Letters that leave stage 5 are delivered to the bin of the lane they leave into. Each bin receives at most one delivery per spin.
- **Base and paid spins.** Each bin shows a fresh postage value before the letter drops. A spin pays the postage of every bin it delivers to.
- **FS seals.** Each stage independently carries at most one FS seal, on a random lane. A seal sits over the stamp and never changes the route. Seals on 3, 4 or 5 stages award Night Shift, Double Dispatch or Lost & Found. The bonus plays after the spin pays.
- **Bonuses** (10 spins, no seals, no retriggers). The bins keep their value for the whole bonus. A delivery pays its bin's current value; after the spin pays, upgrades apply:
  - **Night Shift:** one letter in the middle lane. Each bin that received a delivery rises one rung.
  - **Double Dispatch:** letters enter the top and bottom lanes. Upgrades are shared: every bin rises one rung per delivery.
  - **Lost & Found:** letters enter all three lanes, with one automatic rescue per spin (the first branch that would be lost carries straight on). Upgrades are shared.
  - **Starting values:** the outer bins start one rung above the middle bin.
- **Paid spins:**
  - **Priority Mail** (3×) is the same spin with more FS seals.
  - **Carbon Copy** (1.5×) opens every spin with a forced Fork in stage 1.
  - **Registered Mail** (2.5×) protects the route: every jam and edge loss is rescued.
  - Each paid spin's FS chance is solved for 96%.
- **Buys:** Night Shift 100×, Double Dispatch 250×, Mystery Delivery 400×. Mystery Delivery draws Night Shift 40%, Double Dispatch 35%, Lost & Found 25%.
- **The cap** counts the whole round, the spin included. The bonus ends on the spin that reaches it, and that spin is paid only up to the remaining budget.

## Open design questions

- These are prototype interpretations of the kickoff for Dylan to confirm: shared upgrades are per delivery; Lost & Found inherits shared upgrades; Registered Mail rescues everything; no retriggers in bonuses; the Carbon Copy and Registered Mail prices of 1.5× and 2.5×; and the Mystery chances of 40/35/25.
- Lost & Found's average is set by Mystery Delivery (400× at 96% with those chances). Changing the chances moves it.
- Night Shift has no floor. It pays nothing 1 in 6,814 and under 25× 2.5% of the time. A guarantee (for example, the last spin always delivers) would need a re-fit.
