## How a round settles

- **The board.** 6 reels × 5 rows. Each cell lands a pay symbol, an FS or a multiplier stamp. Pay symbols by rank: KEY, PHONE, PARCEL, ENVELOPE (premiums), then A, K, Q, J, 10.
- **Pays.** 8 or more of a pay symbol anywhere pay. All winners clear together, survivors drop in order and refills fall from the top, until a check pays nothing.
- **Collections.** Every real symbol cleared in a win adds to its collection. Reaching the target is a **clear**: the symbol performs its job and the meter grows. Excess carries. Base collections last one spin; bonus collections carry across free spins.
- **Jobs** run on the settled board, in rank order. Each symbol fires at most 3 times a spin; later clears still grow the meter.
  - 10 sweeps every 10 off the board.
  - J converts every J to the most common visible premium.
  - Q copies the row with the most of the commonest symbol onto the row with the fewest.
  - K turns the reel with the fewest of the commonest symbol wild for the next check.
  - A stops the next low (10, then J, then Q) landing for the rest of the spin.
  - ENVELOPE pays the spin's biggest win again.
  - PARCEL unpacks four copies of the most common visible premium.
  - PHONE delivers the copies missing from the nearest match.
  - KEY opens the vault: a PARCEL unpack, a K wild reel and a multiplier stamp.
  - Wilds join only the most common symbol and leave after one check. Tie-breaks are higher rank, then reel order, top to bottom.
- **The meter.** Stamps add their value as they land. A clear adds its increment (KEY/PHONE +3, PARCEL/ENVELOPE +2, lows +1; *tiered*) or, in Lost & Found, doubles the meter. Per spin: stamps first, then clears, then the final meter multiplies the spin's tumble sum once.
  - **Base and paid spins:** a spin-local meter starting at 1×.
  - **Night Shift:** the meter starts at 1×, carries through the bonus and applies only on a spin with a clear or a stamp. Otherwise wins pay face value.
  - **Double Post:** starts at 10× and applies to every spin.
  - **Lost & Found:** halves the targets, applies to every spin, and doubles per clear up to 64×.
- **FS** on the final board: 4, 5 or 6+ award Night Shift, Double Post or Lost & Found (10 spins). In a bonus, 3+ FS add 5 spins.
- **Paid spins:** Priority Mail (3×) has a higher FS chance. Special Delivery (2×) and Double Exposure (2.5×) open with one or two previewed 2×–25× stamps. Buys: Night Shift 100×, Double Post 250×, Mystery 400× (Night Shift 40%, Double Post 35%, Lost & Found 25%).
- **The cap** is 25,000× the base bet across the whole round. The round ends on the spin that reaches it.

## Open questions for the producer

- **Prototype choices to confirm:**
  - Meter starts: Night Shift 1×, Double Post 10×, Lost & Found 1×.
  - Lost & Found's doubling ceiling is 64×. Without one it averaged far above its price and capped in most bonuses.
  - A filters last one spin. A filter kept for the whole bonus thinned the refills for good and ran away.
  - Bonus targets and the tiered increments.
  - Prices, and Mystery at 40/35/25.
- **Night Shift versus Double Post.** With rare stamps, Night Shift's meter grows mostly through clears, and those also activate it. Its event rule therefore costs little, and Double Post needs a higher starting meter to be worth 2.5× the price.
- **Premium collections almost never clear in Night Shift or Double Post** (KEY, PHONE, PARCEL and ENVELOPE under 0.002 clears per bonus). Premiums rarely make 8+ in the free-spin mix, so the vault, deliver, unpack and return jobs effectively never fire there. That is also why flat and tiered increments measure the same: almost every clear is a low, which is +1 either way. Lowering premium bonus targets to about 8–10, or weighting premiums up in free spins, would make them appear. Either needs a re-fit.
- **Night Shift is swingy.** 42% of bonuses see no clear, the median is 32×, and 44% pay under 25×. Lost & Found reaches its 64× meter ceiling in 51% of bonuses and the 25,000× cap in 1 in 1,344.
- **Job tie-breaks** are listed in `engine/engine.js` and ENGINE.md. Fixtures cover each job, and the example payloads Codex asked for: inactive Night Shift, late activation, Double Post with no event, and Lost & Found with a stamp and a clear in one spin.
