## Rules as the trial engine settles them

- **Board.** The same 6×5 pay-anywhere tumbles. There are no stamps and no collection jobs.
- **Base and Priority Mail spins** have no rack and pay face value. Their FS chance is fitted for 96%.
- **The rack.** Each bonus draws six symbols: three basics and three premiums. Lost & Found always has KEY plus two other premiums. The rack is fixed for the whole bonus.
- **The meter (ladder).** Every rack symbol has its own next addition: basics start at +1 and premiums at +3. Each time that symbol pays, its addition goes on the meter and then doubles for next time. The first KEY match multiplies the whole meter by 2, and every later one by 4. Additions apply before the KEY. The meter carries through the bonus and multiplies each spin's tumble subtotal once.
  - **Night Shift** starts at 1× and applies only on a spin with a rack hit.
  - **Double Post** starts at 10× and applies every spin.
  - **Lost & Found** starts at 1× and applies every spin.
  - Ceilings are 250× / 250× / 1000×.
- **Free-spin landings (fitted).** Night Shift basics ×0.8929, Double Post basics ×0.956, Lost & Found KEY weight 25.2363. Mystery Delivery draws regular 40%, super 35%, hidden 25%.

## Findings for the producer

- **KEY almost never doubles in Night Shift or Double Post** (0.2% and 0.1% of bonuses with KEY racked). Their meters grow from basics and a few premiums. Making KEY land more there would add the doubling, but also variance.
- **Lost & Found's value comes from KEY doubles.** 74% of its bonuses double at least once, it reaches the cap 1 in 114, and its free spins land KEY at weight 25.2363 to get there.
- **Night Shift activates on 31% of its spins.** The rest pay face value.
- **Golden seeds** are in `tests/six.test.mjs`. Each free spin's per-tumble meter events are in `steps[].rack` (`hits`, `add`, `doubled`, `before`, `after`, plus `keyFactor`, `next` and `nextKey` for the ladder).
