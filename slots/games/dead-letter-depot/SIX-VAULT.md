## Rules as the trial engine settles them

- **Board.** The same 6×5 pay-anywhere tumbles. There are no stamps and no collection jobs.
- **Base and Priority Mail spins** first draw a rack of 2 basics. Each paying group of a racked basic doubles that spin's meter, which starts at 1× and multiplies the spin's tumble subtotal once. Their FS chance is fitted for 96%.
- **The rack.** Each bonus draws six symbols: three basics, two of PHONE, PARCEL and ENVELOPE, and KEY. **Every bonus racks KEY.** The rack is fixed for the whole bonus.
- **The meter.** Each racked basic or premium that pays adds to the meter: Night Shift basics add nothing, premiums +1; Double Post basics add nothing, premiums +1; Lost & Found basics +1, then +2, premiums +3, then +6. KEY never multiplies. The meter carries through the bonus and multiplies each spin's tumble subtotal once.
- **The key ring.** Every KEY that lands in a bonus, on the first board or in a refill, turns one notch. Each full ring of 5 notches adds to the meter: Night Shift +1, Double Post +1, Lost & Found +20. Notches carry across free spins.
- **The vault.** The first KEY match (8 or more KEYs paying) opens the vault: the meter goes to its ceiling (1,000× in every bonus) and stays there for the rest of the bonus.
  - **Night Shift** starts at 2× and applies only on a spin with a rack hit, a ring fill or an open vault.
  - **Double Post** starts at 5× and applies every spin.
  - **Lost & Found** starts at 30× and applies every spin.
  - Ceilings are 1000× / 1000× / 1000×.
  - **Lost & Found pays at least 1,000×.** A bonus that ends below it is topped up to it.
- **Free-spin landings (fitted).** Night Shift basics ×1.6837 and KEY weight 5, Double Post basics ×1.9586 and KEY weight 5.5, Lost & Found basics ×1.4582 and KEY weight 9. Mystery Delivery draws regular 0%, super 90%, hidden 10%.

## Findings for the producer

- **KEY acts in almost every bonus.** The ring fills at least once in 97.8% of Night Shift, 98.2% of Double Post and 100.0% of Lost & Found bonuses (1.9, 2.0 and 3.6 fills on average).
- **Buys:** Night Shift (100×) pays back at least its price in 34% of buys and less than half of it in 21%; Double Post (250×) pays back at least its price in 36% of buys and less than half of it in 16%.
- **The vault is the max-win path.** A KEY match opens it in 1 of every 148 Lost & Found bonuses, and Lost & Found reaches the cap 1 in 159. Night Shift and Double Post open it 1 in 6,250 and 1 in 5,181.
- **Lost & Found:** 57% of bonuses pass 1,000× by themselves, and the 1,000× minimum pays the other 43%. The median bonus pays 1,132×.
- **Night Shift applies its meter on 59% of its spins** (53% have a rack hit; the rest of them a ring fill or an open vault). The others pay face value.
- **Golden seeds** are in `tests/six.test.mjs`. Each free spin records its first board's ring event in `land` and each tumble's in `steps[].rack` (`hits`, `add`, `keys`, `fills`, `notches`, `opened`, `vault`, `before`, `after`, `next`). `meter` sums the spin: `fills`, `vault`, `notches`, `hit`, `active`.
