## Rules as the trial engine settles them

- **Board.** The same 6×5 pay-anywhere tumbles. There are no stamps and no collection jobs.
- **Base and Priority Mail spins** have no rack and pay face value. Their FS chance is fitted for 96%.
- **The rack.** Each bonus draws six symbols: three basics and three premiums. Lost & Found always has KEY plus two other premiums. The rack is fixed for the whole bonus.
- **The meter.** In each tumble, every paying group of a rack symbol moves the meter: a basic adds +1, a premium adds +3, and KEY doubles. Additions apply before the doubling. The meter carries through the bonus and multiplies each spin's tumble subtotal once.
  - **Night Shift** starts at 1× and applies only on a spin with a rack hit.
  - **Double Post** starts at 10× and applies every spin.
  - **Lost & Found** starts at 1× and applies every spin.
  - Ceilings are 250× / 250× / 1000×.
- **Free-spin landings (fitted).** Night Shift basics ×1.1226, Double Post basics ×1.1259, Lost & Found basics ×1 and KEY weight 31.1585. Mystery Delivery draws regular 40%, super 35%, hidden 25%.

## Findings for the producer

- **KEY almost never doubles in Night Shift or Double Post** (0.1% and 0.1% of bonuses with KEY racked). Their meters grow from basics and a few premiums. Making KEY land more there would add the doubling, but also variance.
- **Lost & Found's value comes from KEY doubles.** 93% of its bonuses double at least once, it reaches the cap 1 in 373, and its free spins land KEY at weight 31.1585 to get there.
- **Night Shift activates on 39% of its spins.** The rest pay face value.
- **Golden seeds** are in `tests/six.test.mjs`. Each free spin's per-tumble meter events are in `steps[].rack` (`hits`, `add`, `doubled`, `before`, `after`).
