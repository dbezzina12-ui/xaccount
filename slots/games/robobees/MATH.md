> Figures are simulated, not exact. Each paid mode's RTP is its line wins over millions of paid spins (Base spins 32M, Signal Boost 16M, Pollen Charge 8M, Hive Overdrive 2M), plus each bonus's trigger chance times its average (Pollen Charge and Hive Overdrive: whole rounds, as their bonuses keep the trigger spin's multipliers) over 2.0 million Regular, 2.0 million Super and 1.0 million Hidden bonuses. Trigger chances are importance-sampled: each paid spin is shadowed by one from the same state at a 20% FS chance per reel, weighted back to the true chance. **±** is a 95% interval. Hit rates, medians, percentiles and bands come from whole rounds played as the game plays them (Base spins 12M, Signal Boost 6M, Pollen Charge 16M, Hive Overdrive 8M).
