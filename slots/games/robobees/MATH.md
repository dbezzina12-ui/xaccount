> Figures are simulated, not exact. Each paid mode's RTP is its line wins over millions of paid spins (Base spins 32M, Signal Boost 16M, Scout Run 8M, Pollen Charge 8M, Hive Overdrive 2M), plus each bonus's trigger chance times its average over 2.0 million Regular, 2.0 million Super and 1.0 million Hidden bonuses. Trigger chances are importance-sampled: each paid spin is shadowed by one from the same state at a 20% FS chance per reel, weighted back to the true chance. **±** is a 95% interval. Hit rates, medians, percentiles and bands come from whole rounds played as the game plays them (Base spins 12M, Signal Boost 6M, Scout Run 4M, Pollen Charge 4M, Hive Overdrive 1M).
