## How the catalogs are made

**Pools (natural play).** Every pool is real rounds of the shipping engine (`runtime/engine.js`), seeded 1, 2, 3, …:
- **Bonuses:** 2,000,000 of each bonus.
- **Paid spins:** 4,000,000 per mode. A spin with 3 or more scatters is left out of the no-bonus pool; its tier is drawn separately.
- **Trigger spins:** 300,000 per mode and tier, forced to 3, 4 or 5 scatters. Base Spins and Signal Boost share them, because the forced scatters make them the same draws.

**Catalog.** Each pool keeps its shape in 14,000 quantile strata, one seed per stratum weighted by its size (3,000 for trigger spins), plus its top outcomes one by one. The shipped catalog holds 141,218 seeds.

**Shaping.** The weights then bend each pool's natural shape. All of it is in `tools/math/cyber-skunk-catalogs.mjs`, `SHAPE`:
- **Vent Break:** natural average 104.76x. A log-space tilt (curvature −0.05) brings it to exactly 96x and lifts the median from 16.8x to 19.1x.
- **Overpressure:** natural average 265.89x.
  - A tilt (curvature −0.03) brings it to exactly 240x.
  - Its 25,000x rounds are 64 different rounds: those of the pool plus a search of 20,000,000 more seeds (`cyber-skunk-caps.mjs`). Naturally the bonus reaches the cap only about 1 in 340,000.
- **Cap rates** are design choices (`SHAPE`, `BASE_MAX`): the bought bonuses use Dylan's rates. The triggered pools scale the bought rates by one factor, solved so base play reaches the cap 1 in 1,000,000; the body of each pool is re-tilted so its average stays 96x or 240x.
- **Blackout Protocol:** only its natural rounds of 1,000x or more (15,500 entries from 433,234 such bonuses out of 2,000,000), in their natural shape, with the cap's share set to 1 in 200.
- **No-bonus spins:** each mode's are tilted last, so the whole mode returns exactly 96%: Base 0.48x → 0.46x per spin (θ 0.019), Spray Job 9.39x → 9.12x (θ 0.011), Signal Boost 0.45x → 0.26x (θ 0.224). Signal Boost's bonuses pay 87% of its price (a richer Blackout Protocol, which it triggers 14.9 times as often), so its plain spins pay less.
- **Signal Boost price:** at 3x it needs a scatter chance of 0.1125 per reel, raised from 0.11. At 0.11 it returned 91% at 3x, and reaching 96% would have meant lifting its spin pays by a third.

**Money.** Every pay is a whole 0.1x of the base bet, and every base bet is a whole 10 cents, so every win is exact whole cents: bet × pay / 100. No rounding happens anywhere.
