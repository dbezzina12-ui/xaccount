GAMBOLIGY STUDIO

Your canvas, your game.

Changes are rendered inside the imported Zombify game. Play lets you use its real controls. Desktop and mobile positions are independent; symbols and reel settings are shared.

Editing

Drag layers to move them. Use the bottom-right handle to resize. Arrow keys nudge by 1 px; Shift moves by 10 px. Ctrl/⌘ Z undoes, Ctrl/⌘ S saves. Projects autosave in this browser; export a project to move it elsewhere.

Alignment & presets

Scene has snap and safe-area guides. Align & Link in the inspector centres or aligns a layer, and Move with links its position to another layer. In Screens, Edit whole group moves a panel with its text. On phones, the preview stays pinned while you edit; untick Pin phone preview for more space.

Presets saves cabinet layouts, sound packs and bonus screens in this browser. Export and import a preset to move it to another compatible Studio project. Included artwork and audio travel with the preset. Applying a preset is one Undo step.

Grid compatibility

Zombify’s current game logic uses 6 × 5. Other grids use the reusable engine’s reel animation for layout tests. They do not place bets or calculate payouts. A new game adapter is required to play those grids.

Bring in artwork

Create artwork in your ChatGPT conversation, then use Assets → Upload and assign it to a background, layer or symbol. For backgrounds, attach the image with Quick edit’s ＋ button and apply “change background”. Select Desktop or Mobile first. Export your project to move saved edits between devices.

Commands & agents

Quick edit supports local commands such as “grid 5×4”, “symbols 115%”, “center reels”, “blur 3” and “saturation 120”. Attach an image and use “change background”. No AI provider is connected to this text box.

In a supported ChatGPT browser, the studio exposes read_slot_studio and edit_slot_studio tools. An agent can inspect and edit this open project with the same validation and undo history as the visible controls.

Original mechanics

This is a free-play development tool. Visual edits do not retune payouts. Imported Zombify outcomes are unchanged; this editor is not a math certification or a production release.
